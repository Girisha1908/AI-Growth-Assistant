# Brag plan: Lenny Growth Assistant

**What it is:** a chat assistant that answers product and growth questions only from Lenny's Podcast transcripts. It cites the guest and the exact second, refuses when the transcripts don't cover a question, and runs locally on Ollama.

**For whom:** early-career PMs and growth people who learn from Lenny's Podcast but can't search 303 episodes.

**Angle:** "Ask the guests, get the receipts." Trust is the differentiator: citations to the second, and an honest refusal.

**Hook:** the scale problem. *303 episodes. 4.6 million words. Nobody's reading all of that.* This sits over faint, scrolling real transcript lines.

**Tone:** default (punchy, clean), in the app's own "Starry Night" identity: violet canvas `#151121`, lavender accent `#9d85e0`, EB Garamond for headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans for UI text.

**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.
